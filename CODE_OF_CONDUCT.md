# Code of Conduct

Click2Whoosh is a small hobby project. Keep it a friendly place to report bugs, share firmware
results and help with translations.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Bluetooth, Python or GitHub.
- Assume good faith, and give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Write issues and pull requests in English or Italian.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or anything unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
do not follow these rules. To report a problem, use a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through the
profile linked from this repository.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
